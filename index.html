Target = the shortest price worth taking for a win by at least that many lengths (jumps 2/4/6/8, flat 1–5), treating the current price as the SP. It trusts the model's view only as far as it held up on 2023–26 races and adds a safety margin (10% at the shorter margins rising to 20% at the longest). Runners are listed in price order. Struck through: hurdle favourites the model marks down at 8L, which lost money in testing. Green: the model rates this runner's chance clearly above what its price alone implies. Orange: clearly below. Small figure: estimated chance. "From" is the first on-course show price; before the show the page only shows Sporting Life's tissue for reference and treats the price as unmoved. Dashed row: chance the race is won by at least that margin, whoever wins. "If it wins": the chance this runner's win would be by at least each margin. GB jumps, flat turf and all-weather. Bumpers have no targets but count in the meeting total (treated as hurdles). Meeting total: the last chip in each course's race list.
